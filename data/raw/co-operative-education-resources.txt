Supports and resources
Co‑operative and Experiential Education (CEE) offers a range of services and supports to help you navigate your co‑op journey with confidence. Whether you’re exploring opportunities, adjusting your plans or managing challenges, you’ll find guidance and resources to support your well‑being, success and sense of connection every step of the way.

Connect with your co-op advisor
Your co-op advisor is here to support you before, during and after your work term. Whether you have questions, need guidance or want to explore your options, your advisor can help connect you with the right resources and next steps.
Connect with your co-op advisor by sending them a message on WaterlooWorks. If you’re heading into an international (non‑U.S.) work term, you may be matched with a specialized advisor to support your experience abroad.
