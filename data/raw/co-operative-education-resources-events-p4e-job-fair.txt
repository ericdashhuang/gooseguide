P4E Job Fair
You are invited to the largest job fair of its kind in the country!
Partnerships for Employment (P4E) hosts the largest job fair events of its kind for students in the country. P4E Job Fairs are a collaborative effort among the University of Waterloo, Wilfrid Laurier University, University of Guelph and Conestoga College to connect employers with students. Events happen twice a year, in winter and fall terms.
P4E has over 30 years of experience connecting employers and students for co-op and full-time roles.
