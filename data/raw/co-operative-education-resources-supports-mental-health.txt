TITLE: Mental health resources

Mental health resources
Navigating the co-op process on top of schoolwork and other life demands can be challenging. Whatever you're experiencing, we're here to support you and are committed to helping you have the most positive experience possible.
All of our co-op advisors have received mental health training through Counselling Services and can provide support and direct you to other services on campus or in your region.
Co-op embedded wellness counsellors
Co-op embedded wellness counsellors are here to support your mental health throughout your co-op journey, whether you’re preparing for a work term, on the job or navigating recruitment.
They offer confidential, one-on-one support, including virtual or phone appointments. They understand the unique pressures of co-op and can help you work through challenges related to both school and work.
You can reach out for help with things like:
Managing stress
Anxiety
Relationship or family challenges
Grief and loss
Adjusting to change (e.g., starting a co-op or returning to school)
Workplace challenges
Anything you share will remain confidential and will not be shared with your co-op advisor or other staff.
Campus Wellness
Counselling Services: Individual appointments - Available at no charge to registered University of Waterloo students in classes or on co-op terms.
Group therapy - Counselling Services offers several group therapy options which can provide you with a supportive environment.
Seminars and workshops - Help enhance your personal success by equipping you with strategies, tips and skills to help you cope with life's challenges.
Helplines Good2Talk is a post-secondary helpline that offers free, confidential counselling available to all Ontario post-secondary students between ages 17-25. Here 24/7 is a helpline that helps people in Waterloo Region access addictions, mental health and crisis services provided by 12 agencies across the Waterloo Wellington region. If you are looking for urgent help, please visits Campus Wellness: Urgent help and emergency contacts .
