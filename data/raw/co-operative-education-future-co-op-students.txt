TITLE: Future co-op students

Future co-op students
You belong here. Explore co-op, gain experience and shape your future.
Waterloo's co-op program is a great way to learn how to successfully interview for jobs, see how your degree relates to real-life problems, graduate with up to two years of valuable experience and make money.
With more than 8,000 employers, Waterloo co-op students work in organizations worldwide.
Every student’s co-op journey is different depending on program, interests, goals and work experiences. Learn more about possible co-op pathways and sample co-op jobs by exploring your faculty or program of study.
